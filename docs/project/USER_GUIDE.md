## Getting Started

### Overview
The Interview Feedback Tracker helps you systematically collect, analyze, and improve your interview performance. It integrates seamlessly with your existing Job Application Tracker to provide comprehensive insights into your job search progress.

### Key Features
- **Structured Feedback Collection**: Use templates to capture consistent feedback across all interviews
- **Performance Analytics**: Visual dashboards showing trends and improvement areas
- **Mobile Support**: Capture feedback immediately after interviews on any device
- **Mentor Collaboration**: Securely share feedback with career coaches and mentors
- **Automated Insights**: AI-powered recommendations for skill development

### Prerequisites
Before using the Interview Feedback Tracker, ensure you have:
- Access to the Job Application Tracker
- Appropriate permissions (Interview Feedback User or Manager)
- A job application record to associate feedback with

## Collecting Interview Feedback

### Step 1: Navigate to Interview Feedback
1. Open your Job Application record
2. Scroll to the "Interview Feedback" related list
3. Click "New Interview Feedback" button

### Step 2: Select Interview Type
1. Choose from available templates:
   - **Technical Interview**: Focus on coding, system design, technical skills
   - **Behavioral Interview**: Emphasis on soft skills, experience, culture fit
   - **Leadership Interview**: Management scenarios, team leadership, vision
   - **General Interview**: Balanced assessment across all areas

### Step 3: Fill Basic Information
**Required Fields:**
- **Interview Round**: Specify which round (e.g., "Phone Screen", "Round 1", "Final Round")
- **Interview Date**: When the interview took place
- **Duration**: Length of interview in minutes
- **Interviewer Name**: Primary interviewer's name
- **Interview Type**: Select from the dropdown

**Optional Fields:**
- **Interviewer Email**: For follow-up communications
- **Interview Location**: Physical or virtual location details

### Step 4: Provide Ratings
Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5 (1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent):

- **Overall Rating**: Your general impression of how the interview went
- **Technical Rating**: Performance on technical questions and challenges
- **Communication Rating**: How well you articulated your thoughts
- **Cultural Fit Rating**: How well you connected with the company culture

### Step 5: Add Detailed Feedback
**Detailed Feedback Section:**
- Describe the interview flow and key questions asked
- Note your responses and how well you handled each topic
- Include any technical challenges or case studies discussed

**Strengths Section:**
- List areas where you performed well
- Highlight positive feedback received
- Note skills that were well-received

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Identify topics you struggled with
- Note questions you couldn't answer fully
- List skills to develop before future interviews

### Step 6: Set Recommendation
Based on your assessment, select:
- **Hire**: You believe you performed well and expect a positive outcome
- **Maybe**: Mixed performance with both strengths and weaknesses
- **No Hire**: You believe the interview didn't go well

### Step 7: Save and Review
1. Click "Save" to store your feedback
2. Review the automatically calculated competency ratings
3. Add any additional competency-specific comments if needed

## Viewing Performance Dashboard

### Accessing the Dashboard
1. Navigate to the "Interview Performance" tab in your app
2. Or click "View Dashboard" from any Interview Feedback record

### Dashboard Components

#### Performance Overview
- **Success Rate**: Percentage of interviews leading to next rounds
- **Average Ratings**: Your mean scores across all competency areas
- **Interview Count**: Total interviews completed and feedback collected
- **Trend Indicator**: Whether your performance is improving, declining, or stable

#### Competency Radar Chart
The radar chart shows your relative strengths across key areas:
- **Technical Skills**: Programming, system design, domain knowledge
- **Communication**: Articulation, listening, presentation skills
- **Problem Solving**: Analytical thinking, creativity, approach to challenges
- **Cultural Fit**: Values alignment, team collaboration, company understanding
- **Leadership**: Management potential, influence, strategic thinking

**How to Read the Chart:**
- Larger areas indicate stronger performance
- Compare your profile to industry benchmarks (shown in gray)
- Focus improvement efforts on smaller areas

#### Performance Trends
The trend line chart displays your ratings over time:
- **X-axis**: Interview dates
- **Y-axis**: Rating scores (1-5 scale)
- **Multiple Lines**: Different competency areas
- **Trend Analysis**: Statistical significance of improvements/declines

#### Recent Feedback Summary
Quick access to your last 5 interview feedback records:
- Interview date and company
- Overall rating and recommendation
- Key strengths and improvement areas
- Quick links to detailed feedback

### Filtering and Customization
- **Date Range**: Filter dashboard data by specific time periods
- **Company Filter**: Focus on specific companies or interview types
- **Competency Focus**: Highlight specific skill areas
- **Export Options**: Download charts and data in PDF, Excel, or CSV formats

## Mobile Feedback Capture

### Accessing Mobile Interface
1. Open Salesforce mobile app or mobile browser
2. Navigate to your Job Application
3. Tap "Quick Feedback" for streamlined mobile entry

### Mobile-Optimized Features

#### Quick Rating Sliders
- Swipe-based rating input for faster data entry
- Large touch targets optimized for mobile screens
- Haptic feedback for rating confirmation

#### Voice-to-Text Input
1. Tap the microphone icon in any text field
2. Speak your feedback naturally
3. Review and edit the transcribed text
4. Save when satisfied with accuracy

#### Photo Attachments
- Capture whiteboard solutions or technical diagrams
- Attach interview materials or notes
- Automatic compression for faster upload

#### Offline Capability
- Continue entering feedback without internet connection
- Data automatically syncs when connection is restored
- Visual indicators show sync status

### Mobile Best Practices
- **Immediate Capture**: Enter feedback within 30 minutes of interview completion
- **Voice Notes**: Use voice-to-text for detailed observations while walking to your car
- **Photo Documentation**: Capture any visual elements from the interview
- **Quick Ratings**: Use sliders for fast competency assessment

## Sharing Feedback with Mentors

### Creating Secure Shares
1. Open your Interview Feedback record
2. Click "Share with Mentor" button
3. Enter mentor's email address
4. Select access level:
   - **Read Only**: View feedback and ratings
   - **Comment**: Add suggestions and advice
   - **Full Access**: Edit ratings and feedback (use cautiously)

### Setting Share Parameters
- **Expiration Date**: When access should automatically expire
- **Data Masking**: Hide sensitive information like company names
- **Notification Preferences**: Email alerts for mentor comments

### Mentor Collaboration Features
- **Comment Threads**: Mentors can add specific advice on each section
- **Improvement Suggestions**: Structured recommendations for skill development
- **Resource Sharing**: Links to relevant learning materials or practice resources
- **Progress Tracking**: Mentors can track your improvement over time

### Managing Shared Access
- **Active Shares**: View all current sharing arrangements
- **Access Logs**: See when mentors accessed your feedback
- **Revoke Access**: Immediately remove sharing permissions
- **Extend Expiration**: Prolong access for ongoing mentorship

## Analytics and Insights

### Automated Analysis
The system automatically analyzes your feedback to provide:

#### Performance Trends
- **Improvement Trajectory**: Statistical analysis of rating changes over time
- **Consistency Metrics**: How stable your performance is across interviews
- **Peak Performance**: Identification of your strongest interview types

#### Competency Analysis
- **Strength Identification**: Your top-performing skill areas
- **Improvement Opportunities**: Skills with the most growth potential
- **Skill Gaps**: Areas significantly below industry benchmarks
- **Development Priorities**: Recommended focus areas for maximum impact

#### Pattern Recognition
- **Interview Type Performance**: How you perform in different interview formats
- **Company Size Correlation**: Performance variations by company size
- **Time-of-Day Effects**: Whether interview timing affects your performance
- **Preparation Impact**: Correlation between preparation time and outcomes

### Predictive Analytics
Based on your historical data, the system provides:
- **Success Probability**: Likelihood of advancing to next rounds
- **Optimal Interview Types**: Formats where you're most likely to succeed
- **Skill Development ROI**: Which improvements will have the biggest impact
- **Interview Readiness Score**: Overall assessment of your current capabilities

### Benchmarking
Compare your performance against:
- **Industry Standards**: Anonymous aggregate data from similar roles
- **Experience Level**: Peers with similar years of experience
- **Geographic Region**: Local market performance indicators
- **Company Tier**: Performance expectations for different company sizes

### Actionable Recommendations
The system generates specific, actionable advice:
- **Study Topics**: Specific technical areas to focus on
- **Practice Scenarios**: Behavioral questions to prepare for
- **Resource Links**: Relevant courses, books, and practice platforms
- **Timeline Suggestions**: Recommended preparation schedules

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Cannot Create Interview Feedback
**Problem**: "New Interview Feedback" button is not visible
**Solution**: 
1. Verify you have Interview Feedback User permissions
2. Ensure you're viewing a Job Application record
3. Contact your administrator if permissions are missing

#### Ratings Not Calculating Correctly
**Problem**: Competency ratings don't match your input
**Solution**:
1. Check that all required fields are completed
2. Verify rating values are between 1-5
3. Save the record and refresh the page
4. Contact support if calculations still appear incorrect

#### Dashboard Not Loading
**Problem**: Performance dashboard shows loading spinner indefinitely
**Solution**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Clear browser cache and cookies
3. Try accessing from a different browser
4. Verify you have feedback records to display

#### Mobile Sync Issues
**Problem**: Offline feedback not syncing to server
**Solution**:
1. Ensure you have stable internet connection
2. Force close and reopen the mobile app
3. Check available storage space on device
4. Try manual sync from settings menu

#### Sharing Links Not Working
**Problem**: Mentors cannot access shared feedback
**Solution**:
1. Verify the share link hasn't expired
2. Check that mentor's email address is correct
3. Ensure mentor isn't blocked by company firewall
4. Regenerate share link if necessary
